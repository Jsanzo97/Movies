# Privacy Policy for Movies App

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in the Movies App.

## 1. Information We Collect
The Movies App does not collect or share any personal information. We do not require users to create an account or provide any personal data to use the application.

## 2. Third-Party Services
We use The Movie Database (TMDB) API to provide movie information. We also use Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) to improve the app's performance and stability. These services may collect information such as device identifiers and usage data according to their own privacy policies.

## 3. Local Storage
The app may store user preferences (like layout mode) locally on your device using Android DataStore. This data remains on your device and is not sent to any server.

## 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through the GitHub repository.
